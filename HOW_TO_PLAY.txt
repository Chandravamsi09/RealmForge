1. GAME OBJECTIVE & CORE RULES
================================================================================
RealmForge is an authoritative, real-time multiplayer tower defense game running
on a 20 Hz deterministic Entity Component System (ECS) with 50ms tick rate.

CORE OBJECTIVE:
  - Defend your NEXUS BASE against continuous, procedurally scaling creep waves.
  - Strategically place, upgrade, and synergize defensive towers along the path.
  - Manage your gold treasury, leverage compound interest, and execute timely abilities.
  - If your Nexus HP reaches 0, your realm collapses and the match concludes.

================================================================================
2. GAME MODES
================================================================================
[1] SOLO SURVIVAL (SOLO)
    - Single-player endurance against infinite monster waves.
    - Test new tower upgrade branches, maze configurations, and ability timings.

[2] 2-PLAYER CO-OP (COOP_2P)
    - 2 Tacticians cooperate on a shared battlefield with a shared Nexus.
    - Shared creep bounty gold ensures collaborative synergy (e.g. frost slowing
      combined with heavy mortar artillery).

[3] 4-PLAYER SQUAD RAID (COOP_4P)
    - 4 Tacticians defend against massive creep density and amplified Titan bosses.
    - Enemy health scales dynamically with player count (+60% HP per added player).

[4] 1v1 PVP DUEL (PVP_1V1)
    - Head-to-head competitive tower defense.
    - Spend gold to summon creep waves onto your opponent's lane while defending
      your own. Sending creeps boosts your passive wave income!
    - First player whose Nexus reaches 0 loses; winner gains ranked ELO.

5. BUILDING, PLACEMENT & MAZING RULES
================================================================================
- Tile Grid System:
    The map consists of a 2D tile matrix with 32x32 pixel cells.
    * GREEN Tile: Buildable ground.
    * RED Tile: Obstructed path, existing tower, or illegal placement.
- Anti-Blocking Pathfinding Law (A*):
    Creeps calculate dynamic paths using A* with a Min-Heap priority queue.
    The game engine STRICTLY PREVENTS you from placing any tower that would
    completely block all valid routes from any spawn portal to the Nexus.
    You can create long, winding mazes, but an open path must ALWAYS exist!
- Tower Collision:
    Only 1 tower can occupy a single grid cell. Towers cannot be stacked.

8. TOWER TARGETING SYSTEM & PRIORITY RULES
================================================================================
Every offensive tower can have its targeting behavior customized at any time.

AVAILABLE TARGET PRIORITIES:
  1. FIRST (Default): Attacks the enemy closest to reaching the Nexus (highest path progress).
  2. LAST: Attacks the enemy furthest from the Nexus (closest to the spawn portal).
  3. STRONGEST: Attacks the enemy with the highest current HP (essential for Titan bosses).
  4. WEAKEST: Attacks the enemy with the lowest current HP (eliminates low HP stragglers).
  5. NEAREST: Attacks the enemy closest in Euclidean pixel distance to the tower.
  6. FASTEST: Attacks the enemy with the highest movement speed (targets Swarms and Flyers).

HOW TO CHANGE TARGET PRIORITY:
  - In-Game UI: Select any tower and click the Priority Dropdown in the Right Inspector.
  - Keyboard Shortcut: Select a tower and press [T] or [Tab] to cycle through priorities.

TACTICAL RECOMMENDATIONS:
  - Archer Snipers -> Set to STRONGEST or FASTEST to pick off high-threat targets.
  - Heavy Cannons  -> Set to FIRST to hit the dense lead cluster.
  - Frost Obelisks -> Set to FIRST to ensure frontrunners are chilled immediately.

11. COMBAT & DAMAGE MATHEMATICS (WITH NUMERICAL EXAMPLES)
================================================================================

1. PHYSICAL DAMAGE & ARMOR DIMINISHING RETURNS:
     Reduction % = Armor / (Armor + 100)
     Final Damage = Raw Damage * (1 - Reduction %)

     * Example A: Archer deals 100 Physical Damage to an enemy with 100 Armor.
       Reduction = 100 / (100 + 100) = 50%
       Final Damage = 100 * (1 - 0.50) = 50 Damage.

     * Example B: Archer deals 100 Physical Damage to an enemy with 25 Armor.
       Reduction = 25 / (25 + 100) = 20%
       Final Damage = 100 * (1 - 0.20) = 80 Damage.

2. MAGICAL DAMAGE & MAGIC RESISTANCE (MR):
     Reduction % = min(90%, MR) / 100
     Final Damage = Raw Damage * (1 - Reduction %)

     * Example: Arcane Spire deals 200 Magic Damage to a Golem with 80% MR.
       Reduction = 80%
       Final Damage = 200 * (1 - 0.80) = 40 Damage.

3. LIGHTNING DAMAGE (HYBRID PENETRATION):
     Ignores 50% of the target's Armor and Magic Resistance!
     Effective Armor = Armor * 0.50

     * Example: Tesla Coil deals 100 Lightning Damage to a Dread Ironclad with 120 Armor.
       Effective Armor = 120 * 0.50 = 60 Armor.
       Reduction = 60 / (60 + 100) = 37.5%
       Final Damage = 100 * (1 - 0.375) = 62.5 Damage (instead of 45.4).

4. FROST CHILL DAMAGE SYNERGY:
     Frost attacks deal standard damage, PLUS an automatic +25% multiplicative
     bonus damage if the target is ALREADY slowed by ANY frost source!
     Final Frost Damage = Raw Damage * 1.25 * (1 - Armor Reduction)

5. TRUE DAMAGE:
     Bypasses 100% of Armor and Magic Resistance (Pure unmitigated HP loss).

12. STATUS EFFECTS & CROWD CONTROL RULES
================================================================================
- SLOW (Frost / Abilities):
    Reduces enemy movement speed by specified percentage.
    * Stacking Rule: Slows from multiple towers do NOT additively stack; instead,
      the HIGHEST slow percentage takes priority and refreshes duration.
- STUN (Tesla / Frost Tier 4 / Meteor):
    Completely halts enemy movement (0 px/s).
    * Repeated Stuns refresh the stun timer.
    * Boss Titan Stun Resistance: Bosses possess 50% Tenacity (Stun durations are halved).
- BURN (DoT Magic Damage):
    Deals ticking magic damage every 500ms for duration.
    * Respected by Magic Resistance.
    * Applying a new burn refreshes the timer.
- ARMOR SHRED (Void Singularity):
    Reduces the enemy's effective Armor and Magic Resistance by 35% to 60%.
    Allows physical snipers and cannons to deal near-true damage!

13. WAVE GENERATION & MATHEMATICAL SCALING SYSTEM
================================================================================
Waves scale infinitely using deterministic mathematical curves:

1. HEALTH SCALING:
     HP(Wave, Players) = BaseHP * [1 + 0.22*(Wave - 1) + 0.04*(Wave - 1)^1.5] * [1 + 0.60*(Players - 1)]
     * Wave 1 Goblin: 120 HP
     * Wave 10 Goblin (Solo): 120 * (1 + 1.98 + 1.08) = 487 HP
     * Wave 20 Goblin (Solo): 120 * (1 + 4.18 + 3.31) = 1,019 HP

2. ARMOR & MR SCALING:
     Armor(Wave) = BaseArmor * [1 + 0.08*(Wave - 1)]
     MR(Wave) = min(85%, BaseMR * [1 + 0.05*(Wave - 1)])

3. BOUNTY GOLD SCALING:
     Bounty(Wave) = BaseBounty * [1 + 0.04*(Wave - 1)]
     * Wave 1 Bounty: 15g
     * Wave 20 Bounty: 15 * (1 + 0.76) = 26g

4. BOSS CADENCE:
     Gargantuan Titan Bosses spawn every 5 waves (Wave 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30...).
     Boss waves feature high-tier elite escorts alongside the Titan.

5. WAVE TIMING:
     - 10-Second preparation timer between waves.
     - Players can build, upgrade, sell, and inspect during preparation.
     - Clicking "Start Wave Early" instantly spawns the wave and grants +15g Early Bonus.

14. ECONOMY, GOLD MANAGEMENT, INTEREST & BONUSES
================================================================================
- Starting Gold: 450 Gold.

- Compound Interest Formula:
    At the end of each cleared wave, you receive 10% interest on your unspent gold,
    capped at 50 Gold maximum.
    * 200 Gold held -> +20 Gold Interest
    * 350 Gold held -> +35 Gold Interest
    * 500+ Gold held -> +50 Gold Max Interest

- Wave Clear Bonus:
    Awarded automatically upon eliminating the last creep of a wave:
    Clear Bonus = 50g + (10g * WaveNumber)
    * Wave 5 Boss Clear Bonus: +150g extra flat bonus.

- Flawless Streak Bonus:
    Clearing consecutive waves without taking any Nexus damage adds a streak multiplier:
    +15g per streak count (up to +75g max streak bonus).

- Tower Selling Refund Formula:
    Refund = floor(Total Invested Gold * 0.75) (75% Refund)
    * Example: Base Cannon (200g) + Tier 2 Howitzer (200g) = 400g Invested.
      Sell Refund = 400 * 0.75 = 300 Gold.
    * Selling removes the tower immediately and reopens the tile for construction.
    * Selling does NOT penalize your match score.

21. RANKED LEADERBOARDS & ELO (K=32) FORMULA
================================================================================
Ranked matches adjust player ratings using the official FIDE ELO formula (K=32):
  Expected Score Ea = 1 / [1 + 10^((Rb - Ra) / 400)]
  New Rating Ra' = Ra + 32 * (ActualScore - Ea)

22. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ) & TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================
Q: Why can't I place a tower on a ground tile?
A: Either you have insufficient gold, an entity already occupies the tile, or placing
   the tower would violate the Anti-Blocking Law by sealing off all routes to the Nexus.

Q: What happens if I disconnect mid-match?
A: RealmForge's authoritative server saves your player session. Simply refresh
   http://localhost:3000 to reconnect; the client will download the authoritative
   state snapshot and resume without penalty.

Q: Does selling a tower restore 100% of my gold?
A: No, selling refunds 75% of the total gold invested (Base cost + all upgrade costs).
